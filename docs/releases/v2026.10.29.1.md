# v2026.10.29.1 — GFPDIVSV 1.0 CUDA

Adds the CUDA factor-removal sieve for `n!/d±1` and `n#/q±1`, with shared
dual-sign work, exact product encoding, independent factor checks and checked
self-contained continuation. Offline converters produce standalone GFPPS
expressions or canonical two-line task files.

Only CUDA executables are distributed: Windows/Linux x86-64, each with
sm_86, sm_89, sm_100 and sm_120 targets. No stand-alone host-only sieve is included.
Necessary host preprocessing and exact verification remain part of the CUDA program.
Linux packages statically link GMP6.3.0 and carry corresponding upstream source;
primesieve12.x libraries and their copyright notices accompany both platforms.

Build/startup and physical-device validation are reported separately in the
manifest. A sieve survivor is not a primality proof. Interruptions, errors and
bounded `PARTIAL` runs must not be interpreted as completed searches.

The other ten CUDA programs and their binaries are unchanged; use their
[v2026.10.29 and earlier releases](https://github.com/AstralPrisma/cuda-PRP-sieve-and-check-tools/releases/tag/v2026.10.29).
GFPS remains the generic checker. Private fixed-GFN16 and recursive-Fermat
programs are not added. No website/client update is part of this release.
